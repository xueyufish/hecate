# Expense Reimbursement Procedure (v2.1)

Standard reimbursement limits and the approval matrix for expense claims.

<!-- anchor: sec-limit-standard -->
The standard per-claim reimbursement limit is 800 credits. Claims above 800
credits require pre-approval from the budget owner before submission. Claims
above 5000 credits are escalated to the finance committee and cannot be
approved by a single manager.

<!-- anchor: sec-approval-table -->
| Claim band (credits) | Approver | SLA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 - 800 | Line manager | 2 days |
| 801 - 5000 | Budget owner | 3 days |
| 5001+ | Finance committee | 5 days |

Receipts must be attached within 10 business days of the expense date.
